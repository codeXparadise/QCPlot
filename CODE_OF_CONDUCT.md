# QCPlot Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **QCPlot** pledge to make participation in our community an open, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are committed to fostering a respectful, supportive environment across all project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

We encourage behavior that fosters a thriving community:
- **Kindness and empathy** toward all participants.
- **Respect for diverse perspectives**, backgrounds, and methodologies.
- **Constructive, thoughtful feedback** on code, issues, and statistical formulations.
- **Accountability**: Gracefully accepting feedback, owning mistakes, and learning together.
- **User-focused collaboration**: Prioritizing software reliability, statistical accuracy, and usability.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language, inappropriate humor, or unwelcome advances.
- Doxing or publishing others' private contact details without explicit consent.
- Disruptive conduct that undermines open, productive collaboration.

## Scope & Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies to all QCPlot project spaces (including code repositories, pull requests, issue discussions, and project communications).

Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these standards:
- Violations may be reported directly via GitHub issues or to project maintainers.
- All reports will be reviewed promptly, discreetly, and impartially.
- Enforcement actions range from private constructive guidance and formal warnings to temporary suspension or permanent removal from project repositories.
